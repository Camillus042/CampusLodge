What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone number and password.
  • For landlords, agents and hostel managers: ID or proof of ownership, and the room details and photos you upload.
  • For students: your reservations, credit and the reasons you give if you did not like a room.
  • Payment records. Your card details go to Paystack, never to us.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show listings.
  • To process the ₦5,000 booking fee and your credit.
  • To check listings, IDs and reports, and to stop fraud.
  • To send you messages about your account, like confirmations and warnings.
  • We do not sell your information.

Who can see it

  • After you reserve a room, the owner sees your name and phone number so you can meet.
  • Students can see an owner's WhatsApp number on their listings.
  • Admins can see account and payment records to do their work.
  • Paystack handles the payment.
  • If someone commits serious fraud, we may give their details to the police.

Photos and ID uploads

Your ID or proof of ownership is kept private. Only admins can open it, and it is never shown to other users. Listing photos are shown to students on the listing.

Please upload only pictures you have the right to use.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to correct your details.
  • You can ask us to delete your account. Some records, like payments and fraud cases, may be kept for a while.
  • You can stop emails other than important account messages.

Contact

Questions about your information? Send us a message.
