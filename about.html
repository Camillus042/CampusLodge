Why we built it

Finding a room used to mean asking around, paying agents before seeing anything, and hoping the listing was real. We wanted students to see every option in one place.

Every listing is approved by an admin before it goes live. Hostels carry a Verified badge, and landlords and agents who showed an ID or proof of ownership carry a Trusted badge.
