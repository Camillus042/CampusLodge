How booking works

Five steps, one small fee, and clear rules if things do not go to plan.

1

Find a room

Search or browse and contact the owner on WhatsApp or by phone.

2

Reserve it

Pay the ₦5,000 booking fee. The room is held for you for 48 hours.

3

Visit it

Go and see the room. Check the water, light and security.

4

Decide

Like it: pay the rent directly to the owner. Do not like it: tell us why.

5

Move in

The owner confirms your rent and the room is yours.

What happens to the ₦5,000

If Then Details
You take the room Fee is kept The ₦5,000 pays for the hold and the service. Rent goes to the owner.
You do not like it ₦5,000 becomes credit The owner confirms you did not take it. Up to 3 credits a semester.
The owner does not show up ₦5,000 becomes credit Straight away. It does not count toward your 3 credits.
You cancel ₦5,000 becomes credit Counts as 1 of your 3 credits. The room reopens.
You do nothing for 48 hours ₦5,000 becomes credit The room reopens for other students.

Credit, in short

Can I withdraw credit as cash?

No. It can only be used as the booking fee for another room.

Does credit expire?

Not during the semester.

Can I use it on the same owner?

No, credit cannot be used on a room from the owner you got it from.

Where do I see my credit?

In the navbar, on your dashboard and on the My credit page.

Never pay rent through CampusLodge

Rent is paid directly to the owner. We only ever take the ₦5,000 booking fee.
