Using CampusLodge

CampusLodge helps students find hostels and apartments around AKSU. By using the site you agree to these terms.

We connect students with owners. We are not the owner of any room, and the rent agreement is between the student and the owner.

You must be a student, landlord, agent or hostel manager and give true details about yourself.

Accounts

  • Use one account only. Do not share your password.
  • Landlords, agents and hostel managers must be approved by an admin before their listings show.
  • You must tick the Fair Use Agreement when you sign up.
  • We may warn, freeze or ban an account that breaks these terms.

Listings and approval

Every listing from a landlord or agent is checked by an admin before it goes live. Hostel managers are approved once, then their listings go live directly.

Photos, prices and details must be real. The Trusted and Verified badges mean an admin has seen proof. They are not a promise that nothing can go wrong.

We can reject or take down any listing that looks false or breaks these terms.

Booking fee and refunds

  • The booking fee is a flat ₦5,000, paid through the site. It is not part of the rent.
  • When you pay, the room is marked taken and held for you for 48 hours.
  • The full rent is paid directly to the owner at the house, when the key is handed over. Never pay rent before you have seen the room.
  • If you visit and do not like the room, you tell us why. Once the owner confirms you did not take it, your ₦5,000 becomes credit. The same happens if the 48 hours end without a visit.
  • Credit stays for the semester, can only be used to book another room, and cannot be taken out as cash.
  • You can get credit up to 3 times each semester. Credit cannot be used on a room from the same owner.
  • Your ₦5,000 is not given back as cash.

Fair Use Agreement

This is the agreement everyone ticks at sign-up.

Everyone promises

  • The details I give are true.
  • I will only upload real receipts and real photos.
  • I will not make false claims or false reports.
  • I will use one account only.

Landlords, agents and hostel managers also promise

  • I own the room or I have the owner's permission to rent it.
  • My photos and details are real.
  • I will not give a held room to someone else.
  • I will hand over the key on the agreed day and give a receipt for the rent.
  • My ID and phone number may be given to the police if I commit fraud.

Cheating can lead to a warning, frozen credit or a ban. Serious fraud may be reported to the police and can lead to legal action.

Key and rent problems

If the owner will not hand over the key, or gave the room to someone else, press “There is a problem” on My reservation. An admin looks into it, usually within 24 hours, and the owner's other listings are paused while we check.

If it is the owner's fault, your ₦5,000 becomes credit, and it does not count toward your 3 credits.

Rent is paid directly to the owner, outside the site. We cannot get back rent that was paid outside CampusLodge, so keep your receipt and pay only when you have the key.

Reporting a listing

You can report a listing from its page if it looks fake, the price or photos are wrong, the owner asks you to pay outside CampusLodge, or the room is already taken. An admin reads every report and takes the listing down if it breaks these terms.

Please report honestly. False reports break the Fair Use Agreement.

Bans and appeals

Students are dealt with step by step: a warning first, then frozen credit, then a ban. For serious fraud we may skip steps. For landlords, agents and hostel managers an admin decides each case, and a ban removes all their listings. Students who had reserved those rooms get ₦5,000 back as credit.

If you are banned, you can write to an admin from your account to appeal.
